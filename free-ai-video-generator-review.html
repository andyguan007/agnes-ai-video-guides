Batch-generating videos without paying per clip — and not losing tasks the moment the queue fills up.

The common failure pattern

Most free AI video generators work beautifully off-peak and fall apart the moment traffic spikes. You hit HTTP 503 (queue full) or HTTP 429 (rate limited), and the task either dies silently or forces you to click "retry" forever.

What made the difference

The one tool that changed the workflow wasn't a bigger model — it was how failures are handled. Instead of killing a task on the first 503, the built-in AI smart scheduling engine treats 429 and 503 as temporary conditions and keeps the job moving with smart backoff:

  • Multi-key rotation — add several keys and the engine spreads requests across them, so one key cooling down never stalls the whole pipeline.
  • Auto-cooldown on 429 — a throttled key is parked instead of being hammered into a harder limit.
  • Persisted queue — refresh the page and your tasks are still there; interrupted tasks resume and re-queue automatically.

A concrete example

Eight generation tasks in one session. With a naive retry approach, half would have died on the first 503. With the AI smart scheduling engine, all eight eventually completed — each quietly retried until a slot opened, then finished and moved to the "done" area. No babysitting.
